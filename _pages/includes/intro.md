# Research:
Our group focuses on the challenges of computer vision and large models in real-world applications (e.g., precision medicine, intelligent transportation, remote sensing). Specifically, we are interested in building robust visual intelligence systems that can operate reliably under challenging imaging conditions, including image quality enhancement, large-model-assisted data annotation, and fine-grained feature extraction.
